# Gothic

Pointed arches, vertical tracery, and ceremonial serif hierarchy translated into readable architectural interfaces.

Gothic is defined here by pointed architectural frames and vertical tracery. It does not rely on bookish props, leather, metallic sunbursts, or the stepped symmetry of Art Deco.

- [Cathedral Gothic](cathedral/DESIGN.md) — A deep vaulted composition with pointed window frames, narrow serif display type, and warm tracery.
- [Illuminated Folio Gothic](illuminated-folio/DESIGN.md) — A light folio with rubric headings, decorated initials, and pointed margin frames.
- [Stone Cloister Gothic](stone-cloister/DESIGN.md) — A pale architectural court with repeated pointed bays, measured serif titles, and quiet masonry rules.
