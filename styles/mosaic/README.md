# Mosaic

Small tessellated pieces, visible grout, and assembled color fields around calm continuous reading surfaces.

Mosaic uses repeated small tesserae and visible grout to assemble larger fields. It avoids unequal primary-color rails, arbitrary Memphis confetti, and screen-pixel typography.

- [Tessera Court Mosaic](tessera-court/DESIGN.md) — A warm courtyard composition with small tessera borders, inset panels, and continuous reading fields.
- [Tile Panel Mosaic](tile-panel/DESIGN.md) — A crisp assembled panel of repeated geometric tiles, narrow grout channels, and equal modular sections.
- [Shard Gallery Mosaic](shard-gallery/DESIGN.md) — An asymmetric gallery assembled from angular fragments, broad grout, and flat editorial surfaces.
